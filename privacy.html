Privacy Policy

Estate Core Alerts text-message program. Effective November 11, 2026.

This policy covers the Estate Core Alerts text-message program operated by Kevin Hirsch, an independent software developer and sole proprietor ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, when you text START to +1 (947) 777-7408.
  • Your opt-in and opt-out status and the dates they changed.
  • A record of the messages sent to and received from your number.

How we use it

Only to send you the Estate Core Alerts messages you asked for (system alerts and daily status summaries), to honor STOP and HELP requests, and to keep the consent records that mobile carriers require.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number, your text-messaging opt-in data, or your consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared only with the service providers that deliver the messages (Twilio and the mobile carriers), and only so they can deliver them, or when the law requires it.

Messages

Up to 4 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help. Full program terms: Terms & Conditions.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while you are subscribed and for as long as carrier rules require afterward. To ask for your information to be deleted, reply STOP and text +1 (947) 777-7408.

Contact

Kevin Hirsch, Estate Core Alerts. Text +1 (947) 777-7408, or reply HELP to any message.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page with its effective date.
